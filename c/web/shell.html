PICO DISPLAY GAMES

Eight games and toys for a Raspberry Pi Pico, running here in your browser

Loading...

Press the buttons on the board, or the A, B, X and Y keys.
In the menu A and B move and Y opens an app.
To go back to the menu, press Home (or Esc), or hold A and B together for a second, as on the real board.
Leave the menu alone for 30 seconds and the games play themselves.
The same C code runs on the real thing: a Pico and a Pimoroni Pico Display Pack.
